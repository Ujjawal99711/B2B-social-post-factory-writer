Overview
Most brands today have more channels than ever to tell their story and less control over it than ever before.

Supportact exists to change that.

We are a boutique brand consulting and marketing communications firm headquartered in Kolkata. Since 1998, we've worked with businesses across India to help them not just tell their stories, but own them completely.

What makes us different:
We're not a traditional PR agency. Media relationships matter and we have them. But in a connected world, media coverage is just one piece of a much larger puzzle.

The brands that win are the ones that control their narrative across every touchpoint: digital, traditional, owned, earned, and everything in between.

What we do:
Brand Consulting: Strategic brand positioning, narrative development, and messaging architecture. We help you decide what you stand for and how to communicate it consistently.

Marketing Communications: Integrated campaigns that connect your story to the right audiences through the right channels. We don't just generate visibility; we generate engagement.

Digital Marketing: Digital strategies built on your brand foundation, not disconnected tactics. Social, content, search, and performance aligned to your narrative.

The 360° Approach:
We don't believe in siloed marketing. Your brand story should flow seamlessly across every channel where your audience engages. Our 360° approach ensures consistency, coherence, and cumulative impact.

Who we work with:
Growing businesses that need more than a vendor, they need a partner who understands that their brand is an asset to be built, not just a service to be purchased. Companies ready to take control of their narrative.

Where we work:
Headquartered in Kolkata. Operating wherever our clients' stories take us.
